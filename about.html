Our Story

A little piece of Rera, served to you.

Rera comes from Olatorera, my mother's name. Rera's Treat is my way of sharing a piece of her with the world — through food made with care, generosity and a whole lot of love.

The name "Rera" isn't a word you'll find anywhere else — it was coined, built from scratch, just for this. Because some things you're building deserve a name that's never belonged to anyone before you.

That's the same spirit behind everything that leaves our kitchen in Ogijo. Every spring roll, every skewer, every tray of smallchops is made the way a mother makes food for people she loves — with patience, with care, and without cutting corners just because no one's watching.

We started small, the way most good things do: a few trays, a few orders, a lot of word-of-mouth. Today, Rera's Treat shows up at birthdays, owambes, quiet Sunday get-togethers, and the occasional "I just wanted something to snack on" order — because finger foods aren't just party food. They're an excuse to gather, to share, to pass a plate around and let everyone grab a piece.

Why Ogijo?

Because this is home. Ogijo is where Olatorera's kitchen actually lives, and it felt right to build Rera's Treat here first — for the people who are already our neighbours, before we ever think about anywhere else. From Ogijo to Itaoluwo, Shimawa, Odonguyan, and Lukosi, our finger foods, small chops, pasta, and drinks show up wherever people are gathering.
